Teacher / Parent Guide

Each lesson includes a gentle warm-up, a movement activity, a cool-down, and one optional learning check. Repeat, shorten, skip, or adapt any activity. Participation and safe effort matter more than speed, score, flexibility, or comparison.

📘 My PE Learning Evidence

Lesson completion records participation; a correct learning check records a knowledge response. A grown-up can separately document an observation. These records are learning supports, not a diagnosis or a stand-alone claim of physical-skill mastery.
