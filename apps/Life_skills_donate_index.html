A Little Kindness Quest

Help Keep the Learning Adventures Growing!

Artists, teachers, writers, and programmers put lots of care into making these games free for children.

You never need to donate by yourself. You can tell a parent or trusted grown-up that you enjoyed the game and ask whether they would like to help.

What does support help create?

🎨 New Art Characters, worlds, and animations
🎮 New Games Learning adventures and activities
🔊 Friendly Voices Read-aloud and accessibility tools
🛠️ Upgrades Repairs, testing, and improvements
🔒
Parent or Grown-Up Area

Even a voluntary one- or two-dollar gift can help support future repairs, lessons, art, voices, and new games.

💛 Grown-Ups: Support the Free Games

Donations are optional. Children can continue playing whether or not a grown-up donates.
