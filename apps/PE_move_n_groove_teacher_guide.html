Teacher & Caregiver Observation Guide

A low-pressure companion to the 42 playful movement lessons. Use it to notice learning, plan adaptations, and communicate progress. It is an observation aid—not a medical screen, diagnosis, fitness ranking, or formal grading instrument.

Return to Move & Groove Garden

How to use this guide

Observe the learning target.
Look for awareness of space, a chosen movement pattern, safe choices, communication, or reflection. Do not require a particular speed, range of motion, body position, or amount of repetition.
Offer choice and support.
A seated, standing, supported, gesture-based, verbal, or observed demonstration may be appropriate. Respect the child's preference to pause or decline a movement.

Before the activity

Check the floor, route, equipment, weather or outdoor setting, available supports, and adult supervision. Use soft, light, stable equipment. Keep pathways clear.

During the activity

Use short directions, show a model, allow processing time, and offer breaks. Stop the movement if the child reports pain, dizziness, or feeling unsafe; provide appropriate adult support.

Observation scale

Choose the description that best matches what you observed today. The levels are not grades and need not progress in a straight line. Record “not observed” instead of guessing.

RecordObservable description
Beginning to exploreNotices, watches, selects, or tries an element of the activity; may need modeling, time, or a different participation path.
Practicing with supportParticipates in a chosen version with reminders, modeling, stable support, or help organizing the steps.
Practicing with growing independenceRepeats or adapts a chosen movement, follows a familiar cue, or explains a safe choice with fewer reminders.
Applies the idea in a new wayTransfers the idea to another appropriate setting, chooses an adaptation, helps plan a safe activity, or describes what worked.
Not observed todayThe opportunity was not presented, the child chose another pathway, or there was not enough evidence to make an observation.

Core PE learning domains

DomainWhat to look forPossible evidence
Locomotor and spatial awarenessMoves or represents walking, stepping, hopping, crawling, changing direction, or pausing with awareness of space.Uses a clear path, changes direction, marks the path with a gesture, or explains where space is needed.
Balance, body awareness, and mobilityExplores a comfortable position, control, reach, rhythm, or range without being pushed beyond comfort.Uses stable support, chooses a smaller movement, makes a seated version, or communicates “stop.”
Object controlRolls, taps, tosses, catches, kicks, or guides soft equipment toward a clear target.Uses gentle force, notices the path, selects appropriate equipment, or directs another person safely.
Safety and self-regulationResponds to a cue, recognizes an environmental change, requests rest, or chooses a safer route.Pauses on a stop signal, asks for help, checks space, takes a break, or identifies a safe alternative.
Cooperation and boundariesPractices turn-taking, encouragement, shared choices, and respectful personal boundaries.Waits for a turn, uses kind language, leads with a gesture, or respects another person's “no.”
Healthy habits and reflectionNotices enjoyment, rest, hydration, preparation, or how movement feels.Names a favorite movement, identifies a preparation step, asks for water/rest, or shares what felt comfortable.
