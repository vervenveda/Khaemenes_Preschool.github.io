Five Days of Discovery

A free, gently guided Pre-K pathway. Activities can be spoken, signed, pointed to or drawn. No grades, timers, personal information or required login. An adult may keep private observations on paper; this page does not collect them.

Monday · Listen and Notice

Story & Sounds

Read a short story aloud. Invite the child to name a character, point to an object and clap syllables in one familiar word.

Learning evidence: Show or tell one thing remembered from the story.

Open ABC Story Time

Tuesday · Count and Compare

Numbers in Our World

Count 1–5 safe objects together. Move one object at a time, then compare two groups using more, fewer and the same.

Learning evidence: Arrange two groups and explain which has more.

Open Sunny ABCs & 123s

Wednesday · Explore Color

Color & Observation

Find two colors around the room. Name them, notice light and dark examples, and describe what changes when colors are combined in art.

Learning evidence: Create an original two-color drawing and describe the choices.

Open Art Color Studio

Thursday · Feel and Connect

Friendship & Feelings

Use a fictional story about a friend needing help. Model naming a feeling, asking a question and choosing a kind action.

Learning evidence: Role-play a respectful response to a pretend friend.

Open Friendship Garden

Friday · Share and Reflect

My Mini Showcase

Invite the learner to retell a story, count objects, show art and practice a helpful phrase. Let them choose speaking, pointing or drawing.

Learning evidence: A grown-up notes three things the learner tried and one gentle goal for next week.
