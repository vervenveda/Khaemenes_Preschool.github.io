📚Monday · Listen & NoticeStory & Sounds

🌱 Let's discover

Read a short story aloud. Invite the child to name a character, point to an object and clap syllables in one familiar word.

🔢Tuesday · Count & CompareNumbers in Our World

🌱 Let's discover

Count 1–5 safe objects together. Move one object at a time, then compare two groups using more, fewer and the same.

🔢

🎲 Let's try it!

Count a few safe objects together, then arrange them into two little groups.

Choose a playful activity and enjoy it together.

PLAYPLAY
⭐ Show what you noticed

Arrange two groups and show which has more.

🎨Wednesday · Wonder & CreateColor, Nature & Wonder

🌱 Let's discover

Find two colors around the room. Name them, notice light and dark examples, and describe what changes when colors are combined in art.

🎨

🎲 Let's try it!

Choose two colors and make a tiny picture, or point to a detail outdoors.

Choose a playful activity and enjoy it together.

PLAYPLAY
⭐ Show what you noticed

Create an original two-color drawing and describe one choice.

💛Thursday · Feel & ConnectFriendship & Feelings

🌱 Let's discover

Use a pretend story about a friend needing help. Model naming a feeling, asking a question and choosing a kind action.

💛

🎲 Let's try it!

Act out a small helping moment using a toy or puppet.

Choose a playful activity and enjoy it together.

PLAYPLAY
⭐ Show what you noticed

Role-play a respectful response to a pretend friend.

🌟Friday · Share & CelebrateMy Mini Showcase

🌱 Let's discover

Retell a story, count objects, show art and practice a helpful phrase. Choose speaking, pointing, signing, movement or drawing.

🌟

🎲 Let's try it!

Choose two favorite discoveries and share them in a way that feels joyful.

Choose a playful activity and enjoy it together.

PLAYPLAY
⭐ Show what you noticed

A grown-up may remember three things tried and one gentle goal for next week.

💛 A Little Note for Grown-Ups

These lessons are invitations, not assessments. Children may speak, sign, point, move, draw, or simply watch. Participation can be short, repeated, or skipped. Keep observations private if you choose to make them; this page collects no learner records.
