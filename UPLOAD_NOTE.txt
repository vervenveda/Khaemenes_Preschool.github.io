CRECHÈ RICH PUBLIC RESTORATION v3

This package restores the rich, animated Preschool experience rather than replacing it.

UPLOAD THESE FOUR FILES:
Khaemenes_Preschool.github.io/
├── index.html
├── assets/
│   └── preschool-catalog.js
├── mentor/
│   └── index.html
└── mentor-manifest.json

IMPORTANT:
- DO NOT delete /apps/
- DO NOT move or rename any adventure file.
- The root page is based on the intact rich Crechè Family Home (animated ticker, rainbow hero, orbit buttons, learner home, Adventure Rooms, Today’s Pick, all 52 adventures, trail, child/grown-up modes, family dashboard).
- The mentor is based on the intact full Guided Preschool Mentor (Pip/Miri/Nova/Sage, balanced path logic, feelings check-in, hints, play, read-aloud, Family Guide, local-safe fallback).
- Guest Mentor is public and requires no registration.
- Guest progress/settings/plan use sessionStorage and are not academic records.
- Optional learner profile remains available for formal curriculum continuity.
- Academy-wide Family/Learner Registration is only for formal curricula that need persistent assessments, portfolios, certification, transcripts/records, or cross-grade continuity.
