Khaemenes Academy · Pre-K

Notice and describe

Optional offline-friendly instructional companion. No student data is collected, and no quizzes, grades or existing mastery controls are modified.

1 · Explore

Sort three everyday objects by color, shape and use. Invite a child to name one detail and show another way to group them.

2 · Make connections

Tell a short picture-based story with a beginning, action and ending. Ask what changed and why.

3 · Demonstrate understanding

Count and compare two small groups using real objects. Ask which group has more and how the child knows.

Mentor and family guidance

Offer tactile, spoken or visual response options. Observe curiosity and reasoning, not speed or quiz points.

Invite learners to explain how they know, what might change their conclusion, and what they would explore next. Maintain existing course assessments separately.
