Welcome to Khaemenes Kinder Garden!

You can carry your curiosity, favourite adventures, and growing confidence into the next garden. The Kindergarten platform has a 36-week pathway with daily lessons, weekly mastery checks, family tools, and familiar Preschool activities for review.

1 · Keep the same learnerAsk a grown-up to open Enrollment & Placement, find the existing Pre-K child, and choose Placement → Kindergarten (k). Do not register the child again.
2 · Meet the new gardenOpen Kinder Garden and explore its learning spaces and curriculum roadmap.
3 · Guardian release, then first lessonAfter placing the same child into Kindergarten, an authorized grown-up reviews the separate seven-point Kindergarten release before opening formal lessons. The original learner ID and Pre-K keepsake remain unchanged.
