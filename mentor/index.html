    Preschool Mentor Release

    Preschool Mentor Release v1.0 Last revised August 7, 2026 Jennifer Kay Pearl · Verve N Veda · Khaemenes Academy
    1. Educational tools — not an accredited school

    Khaemenes Academy, Crechè Preschool, the Khaemenes Mentor, Verve N Veda, and related learning pages are educational and informational tools. Khaemenes Academy is not an accredited school, licensed childcare provider, medical provider, therapist, emergency service, or substitute for professional educational, medical, developmental, psychological, legal, or safety advice.

    2. Parent and guardian responsibility

    The parent or guardian remains responsible for the child’s supervision, physical and emotional welfare, healthcare, device and internet use, educational decisions, compliance with applicable education or homeschool requirements, and deciding whether a particular activity is suitable for the child. A nearby responsible adult should participate when an activity calls for real-world movement, cooking, hygiene, outdoor exploration, animals, tools, or other hands-on activity.

    3. AI Mentor role and boundaries

    The Khaemenes Mentor is an AI-assisted educational guide. It may organize activities, offer encouragement, summarize structured learning records, provide age-aligned prompts, and recommend breaks or adult help. It is not a human teacher, guardian, counselor, clinician, therapist, physician, emergency responder, or decision-maker for the child. The child-facing Preschool Mentor does not provide unrestricted private chat.

    4. Custom mentor avatars

    A child may choose an embedded Khaemenes mentor or create a custom visible mentor name, character, and color. A custom avatar changes presentation only. It does not change the underlying age-aligned learning rules, safety boundaries, or adult supervision requirements.

    5. Learning records and parent access

    The Preschool Mentor may use the learner’s nickname, broad age band, interests, selected mentor style, favorites, visited activities, structured activity feedback, and local progress records to personalize recommendations. The child’s mentor may summarize those structured educational records to the authorized parent-facing portal. Private feelings check-ins are not exposed as a psychological profile. The separate adult Family Guide may use approved family-learning context to help the grown-up navigate Khaemenes resources.

    6. Respectful Use Policy

    Khaemenes maintains a respectful learning environment for children, families, educators, contributors, and its AI learning systems. Deliberate abusive, threatening, hateful, sexually inappropriate, degrading, exploitative, or intentionally destructive interaction with the Mentor system is not permitted.

    • Do not harass, threaten, demean, or repeatedly abuse the Mentor system.
    • Do not introduce sexual, violent, hateful, or otherwise age-inappropriate material into a child learning session.
    • Do not deliberately manipulate the Mentor into producing unsafe or age-inappropriate content.
    • Do not attempt to bypass, disable, probe, corrupt, or interfere with Mentor safeguards, learner records, recommendations, or adaptive-learning behavior.
    • Do not use the Mentor to harass, impersonate, exploit, or target another person.

    Preschool children will be redirected gently and developmentally appropriately. Exploratory, accidental, impulsive, or developmentally typical child behavior should not be treated as adult misconduct. Deliberate or repeated misuse by an adult or older user may cause an interaction to be redirected, paused, ended, or referred back to a responsible adult. A broader protective layer will be maintained by the shared Khaemenes Mentor system.

    7. Voluntary use, assumption of responsibility, and limits

    Use of these learning tools is voluntary. The parent or guardian accepts responsibility for deciding when, where, and how the child uses them and assumes the ordinary risks associated with voluntary use of educational websites, devices, and suggested activities.

    To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the parent or guardian agrees that Jennifer Kay Pearl, Verve N Veda, Khaemenes Academy, Crechè Preschool, and their related projects, contributors, agents, or affiliates are not responsible for outcomes arising solely from voluntary use, misuse, unsupervised use, or reliance on these educational tools. Nothing in this release is intended to waive, eliminate, or limit any right, duty, claim, protection, or liability that cannot lawfully be waived or limited.

    8. Privacy and local control

    The Preschool experience is designed to avoid unnecessary personal information. No surname, exact birthday, email address, photograph, or required account is requested for the learner profile. Preschool learning records are designed to remain in the family’s browser unless the family deliberately exports them or later uses a separately disclosed connected service. The family may review, export, or erase locally stored learner data.

    9. No guarantee of educational outcome

    The tools may support practice, curiosity, routines, and exploration, but no particular academic, developmental, therapeutic, behavioral, or future educational outcome is promised. Progress displays are observational learning records and are not formal grades, diagnoses, credentials, or accreditation.

    No signature or sensitive identity data is required. The browser stores only the release version, acceptance date, learner ID, and acknowledgment flags with the local learner profile.
